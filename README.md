# CarCare Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 August 2026**

CarCare ("the app") is a vehicle maintenance tracker. This policy explains what
the app collects, why, and what control you have. It is written to be read, not
to be skimmed past.

---

## Who we are

CarCare is operated by Shaik Sadiq. Questions, requests, or complaints:
**md.sadiqk282@gmail.com**.

## The short version

- You can use CarCare without an account. In that mode your data never leaves
  your phone.
- If you create an account, your data is stored in Google Firebase so it can
  sync across your devices.
- We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
- You can delete your data or your entire account from inside the app at any
  time, without contacting us.

## What the app collects

**If you use offline mode:** nothing is transmitted to us. Everything you enter
stays in the app's storage on your device.

**If you create an account, we store:**

- **Account details** — your email address, an authentication identifier, and
  optionally a display name. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive
  your email and name from that provider. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email",
  we only ever see the relay address Apple generates.
- **Vehicle records** — vehicle names, odometer readings, and the photos you
  choose to add.
- **Maintenance and fuel records** — dates, mileage, service types, parts,
  costs, locations you type in, fuel volumes and prices, and any photos you
  attach.
- **Documents and tickets** — photographs of insurance cards, registrations,
  titles, and traffic or parking tickets that you choose to upload, plus the
  expiry dates, fine amounts, and payment dates you enter.
- **Preferences** — units, currency, and appearance settings.

**What the app does not collect:** location data, contacts, advertising
identifiers, browsing history, analytics on how you use the app, or any
information from other apps on your device. The app contains no advertising
and no third-party tracking software.

## Why we collect it

Solely to provide the app's features: storing your records, calculating fuel
economy and service reminders, and syncing your data between your own devices.
We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning
models, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

## Camera and photo library access

The app asks for camera and photo library permission only when you tap a button
to add a photo. Photos are used exclusively as attachments to your own records.
You can decline these permissions and continue using every other feature.

## Where your data is stored

Account data is stored using **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud
Firestore, and Cloud Storage), operated by Google LLC, which acts as our data
processor. Data may be stored on servers outside your country, including in the
United States. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Firebase
privacy documentation at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Access is restricted by security rules so that each account can read and write
only its own records. No other user can access your data.

## How long we keep it

Your data is kept until you delete it. Deleting your account removes your
records and uploaded photos from our systems. Backups and logs held by our
infrastructure provider may persist for a short period afterward as part of
normal operation.

## Your choices and rights

Inside the app, under **Settings**, you can:

- Change or correct any record at any time
- **Delete all your data** while keeping your account
- **Delete your account**, which removes your account and all associated data
- Sign out, or use offline mode to keep data on-device only

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under laws such as PIPEDA
(Canada), the GDPR (Europe/UK), or the CCPA (California) to access, correct,
export, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to lodge a
complaint with a data protection authority. Because the app already gives you
direct access and deletion, most requests can be completed by you instantly. For
anything else, email **md.sadiqk282@gmail.com** and we will respond within
30 days.

## Children

CarCare is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact
us and we will delete it.

## Security

Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS, and stored data is encrypted at
rest by our infrastructure provider. Access is limited by per-user security
rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

## Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the date above and, where
appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use after changes take effect
means you accept the revised policy.
