# CarCare — Delete Your Account and Data

**Last updated: 12 August 2026**

CarCare lets you delete your account and all associated data yourself,
at any time, directly in the app. No request or waiting period is
required.

---

## Delete everything from inside the app

1. Open **CarCare**
2. Tap the **profile icon** in the top-right corner
3. Scroll to the **Delete** section

You have two options:

### Delete all my data
Removes every vehicle, service record, fuel entry, document, ticket, and
photo — both on the device and in the cloud. Your account remains active
and you stay signed in, so you can start fresh.

### Delete my account
Permanently deletes your account **and** all of the data listed above. You
will be signed out and will not be able to sign in again with the same
account. For security, you may be asked to re-enter your password before
this completes.

---

## What is deleted

- Your account and authentication record
- All vehicles, service and repair history, and fuel entries
- All documents, tickets, and fines, including expiry and payment dates
- All photos you uploaded to the cloud
- Your service reminders and app preferences

## What is retained, and for how long

- **Nothing is retained for our own use.** We keep no copy of your data
  after deletion.
- Our infrastructure provider (Google Firebase) may keep deleted records
  in routine system backups and operational logs for a short period,
  typically up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten. This is part
  of normal database operation and the data is not accessible to us.

## Data that was never uploaded

Photographs of identity and ownership documents (driver's licences,
insurance cards, registrations, titles) and photographs of tickets are
**never uploaded**. They remain in the app's private storage on your
device. Deleting the app, or using either delete option above, removes
them from the device. We never held a copy.

## Using CarCare without an account

CarCare can be used entirely offline by choosing **Continue offline** on
the sign-in screen. In that mode no account exists and no data ever leaves
your device — uninstalling the app removes everything.
